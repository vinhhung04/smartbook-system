## Baselines

- **RANDOM** – seeded shuffle of eligible books.
- **POPULARITY** – train-window loan count.
- **V1_PRODUCTION** – current `recommendation.py` (hardcoded weights; semantic=[] offline, as before).
- **V2** – this work. **V2+CATEGORY_CAP3** – diversity constraint. **V2_NO_CANDIDATE_PRUNING** – ranks every eligible book.
- **CONTENT_ONLY** (preference+author+semantic) and **CF_ONLY** (item-item collaborative) – single-family variants, tuned the same way.
- **ORACLE_TRUE_PREFERENCE** – reads the simulator's latent truth (static category preference × popularity × favourite author); a reference ceiling, not deployable. It is not Bayes-optimal: it ignores habit momentum, wishlist boosts and stock, so an observable-data model can approach or exceed it on some metrics.

## Limitations

- The data is synthetic: the simulator's choice model (category preference × Zipf popularity × author affinity) favours signals of that shape; results show what V2 recovers from observable rows, not real-user accuracy.
- 113 books / 11 categories: the candidate pool covers most of the catalog, so candidate generation is exercised but rarely decisive; item-item CF is estimated on a small item set.
- Semantic similarity is TF-IDF over title/author/category/description (same code offline and online); production V1's Qwen embeddings are not evaluated offline.
- Availability is the point-in-time state at the cutoff, while targets span the whole test window.
- Weights are tuned on one validation window of one seed; the bootstrap CI covers user sampling, not dataset/seed variance.
- `feature_scale` is fixed from tuning; in production the popularity/recency count ranges drift as history grows.

## Conclusion

- V2 vs V1_PRODUCTION on 344 test users: hit_rate@10 better (Δ=+0.2820, 95% CI [+0.2238, +0.3430]); ndcg@10 better (Δ=+0.2158, 95% CI [+0.1859, +0.2471]); recall@10 better (Δ=+0.2656, 95% CI [+0.2253, +0.3080]); mrr better (Δ=+0.2216, 95% CI [+0.1744, +0.2671]).
- V2 vs POPULARITY on 344 test users: hit_rate@10 better (Δ=+0.0930, 95% CI [+0.0581, +0.1308]); ndcg@10 better (Δ=+0.0760, 95% CI [+0.0500, +0.1009]); recall@10 better (Δ=+0.0964, 95% CI [+0.0663, +0.1282]); mrr better (Δ=+0.0632, 95% CI [+0.0238, +0.1034]).
- V2 closes 94.1% of the NDCG@10 gap between POPULARITY and the latent-truth ORACLE ceiling.
- Coverage@10 / Personalization@10: POPULARITY 0.7965/0.8537, V1_PRODUCTION 0.9381/0.8856, V2 0.885/0.8946.
- Ablation (NDCG@10, paired CI): removing collaborative, preference significantly hurts; popularity, quality, availability, recency show no significant effect; removing semantic would help.
- Item cold start: Recall@10 on masked new books - V2 with imputation + new-arrival slot 0.1694, imputation only 0.0196, zero evidence 0.0, POPULARITY 0.0, RANDOM 0.1122 (V2 when their history is known: 0.6531).
- Cold start, new users (n=58): NDCG@10 V2 0.4324 vs POPULARITY 0.4288 (V2 higher).
- Cold start, sparse users (n=13, small sample - indicative only): NDCG@10 V2 0.2855 vs POPULARITY 0.4069 (POPULARITY higher).
- Acceptance PASSED - V2 may become the production default.
